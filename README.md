## 🏛 Executive Overview

**Abu Dhabi Airports — Executive AI Agent** is an air-gapped, sovereign executive mobile interface engineered exclusively for C-suite leadership across Abu Dhabi Airports Group and Zayed International Airport (AUH).

### Core Design Philosophy
> *"Design for an executive who has 30 seconds, not an analyst who has 30 minutes."*

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities & Flagship Flows

1. **Executive Today View & 4-Card Swipeable Carousel**:
   - iOS-native horizontal snap-scrolling cards highlighting high-priority morning sessions and regulatory directives from GCAA.
   - 30-second morning briefing with real-time operational KPI indices (On-Time Departures, Baggage Index, Terminal A status).

2. **60-Second Pre-Meeting Briefings**:
   - Scannable, high-leverage meeting briefings (*Purpose*, *What Changed*, *Prior Commitments*, *Open Issues*, *Suggested Strategic Questions*).

3. **Live Elahi Meeting Recording & iOS Dynamic Island Integration**:
   - 1-tap live meeting audio transcription and sovereign real-time decisioning.
   - Dynamic Island transitions to **Elahi Active** with real-time acoustic waveforms and elapsed timer.

4. **Institutional Memory & Recorded Meetings History**:
   - Full history of past executive sessions with multi-speaker diarized verbatim transcripts, automated AI agent decisions, and pending action sign-offs.

5. **Consequence-First Executive Approvals**:
   - Clear human-in-the-loop authorization protocol displaying exact operational consequences before signing.

6. **"Why This Answer?" Provenance & Audit Trail**:
   - Complete data transparency citing internal airport systems (AODB, ELT Calendar, Shift Logs, Q3 Operational Model) with conflict resolution disclosure.

7. **Bilingual Engine (English & True Arabic RTL)**:
   - Full semantic translation and true layout mirroring (`dir="rtl"`) tailored for UAE executive governance.

8. **Executive Presentation Mode (`📽 Present`)**:
   - Fitted viewport mode automatically framing the complete iPhone device mockup with floating glass HUD and keyboard shortcut support (`Escape` key).
